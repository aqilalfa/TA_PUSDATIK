# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Internal BSSN users who need to query, analyze, and verify SPBE regulations and security audit documents. They work in a government/security context where answers must be readable, traceable, and supported by clear source references.

## Product Purpose

This product is an internal Retrieval-Augmented Generation system for SPBE regulations and BSSN audit documents. It helps users ask natural-language questions, receive grounded answers, and inspect citations so they can trust where each answer came from. Success means users can find relevant regulation passages, audit findings, and supporting document details quickly without losing citation accuracy.

## Brand Personality

Formal, clear, trustworthy. The interface should feel calm and professional, prioritizing legibility, source transparency, and task completion over decoration.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style SaaS gloss, decorative cards that reduce readability, cramped citation layouts, low-contrast muted text, playful visual language, and UI patterns that obscure document provenance or legal/audit references.

## Design Principles

- Make evidence inspectable: citations, document titles, sections, and details must remain readable and easy to scan.
- Prioritize clarity over ornament: visual hierarchy should support verification workflows, not distract from them.
- Preserve institutional trust: use calm spacing, stable layouts, and predictable interactions.
- Design for dense Indonesian legal/audit text: long titles and metadata must wrap gracefully without overlap.
- Keep accessibility practical: meet WCAG AA contrast expectations and maintain keyboard-readable, screen-reader-friendly structure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Text contrast must be readable on tinted surfaces, citation cards must support long Indonesian document names without clipping or overlap, and motion should be minimal or respect reduced-motion preferences.
